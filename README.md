# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers.

### Why I Like It
I like this series because it has:
- Interesting characters
- Exciting adventures
- Mystery and suspense
- Friendship and teamwork

### My Favorite Character
**Eleven** is my favorite character because she is brave, strong, and cares about her friends.

### My Favorite Quote

### Rating
**9/10** – I highly recommend watching this series!
